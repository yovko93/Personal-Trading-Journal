# Trading Calendar

## M13.1 — Read-only monthly data

`TradingCalendarQuery(year, month, tradingAccountId?)` selects a displayed New York calendar month. `null` Account means all accounts; an empty Account ID is invalid. `ITradingCalendarReader.GetAsync` returns `TradingCalendarMonth` with `MonthStart`, `GridStart`, `GridEnd`, the complete ordered `GridDates`, and one `TradingCalendarCurrency` per historical pricing currency with matching Trades. The grid begins on Monday on/before the first of the month and ends on Sunday on/after the last. It has four to six complete rows. For example, September 2026 includes August 31 through October 4. Adjacent-month days are marked by `IsInDisplayedMonth` but contribute normally to their visible week's totals. Even an empty month has a complete date grid and no fabricated currency bucket.

The reader delegates **one** inclusive grid-date query to `IDashboardAnalyticsReader`, which filters fully closed Trades by `ClosedAtUtc` using the existing DST-safe New York midnight boundaries. Open and partially exited Trades do not contribute. It does not use Trades-list paging, current Instrument pricing, source import TradeDay, or a retained snapshot. The underlying fresh-context query is no-tracking and projects authoritative persisted Gross, Net, and historical currency, not displayed prices or executions. No database writes are made; cancellation is forwarded to the SQL query and checked during shaping. An Account filter applies to every date and weekly summary. An unavailable Account ID yields an empty result, not another Account's Trades.

Each currency has ordered `TradingCalendarWeek` rows, each with seven `TradingCalendarDay` values and a Monday–Sunday `ClosedTradeMetrics` summary. Weekly totals and closed-Trade counts include **Sunday**, although the UI places the summary in that row's **Saturday cell**. Day and week metrics directly reuse M12's `DashboardMetricCalculator` output. There is no cross-currency monetary total. `Metrics == null` means **no closed Trades** for a day or week; `ClosedTradeCount == 0` is its convenience count, not a financial zero. A populated period with `EffectiveNet.Total == 0` is a genuine zero. `EffectiveNet.Total == null` for a populated period means complete numeric P&L is unavailable; `KnownSubtotal` and `Coverage` remain explicit partial evidence, not a total.

Effective Net uses authoritative Net when costs are known. For a closed Trade with unknown costs and known Gross, it uses Gross as an **estimate** without changing stored cost or Net nullability. `IsEstimated` and `EstimatedTradeCount` retain that provenance at both day and week scope. Unknown Gross cannot become a numeric estimate. Strict verified `Net` and its coverage remain available separately. All amounts retain full decimal precision; the Desktop formats amounts to two culture-aware decimal places without changing those values.

## M13.2 — Month grid and navigation

The Calendar destination now uses a retained `CalendarViewModel` and themed `CalendarView`. It initially selects the current **New York** month, shows a Monday–Sunday grid with four to six rows, and includes dimmed adjacent-month dates. Today has an accent border. Previous, Next, and Today controls have accessible names; day cells are keyboard-focusable and announce their full date, adjacent-month/current-day state, and Saturday summary area. At narrow widths the grid scrolls horizontally instead of compressing seven columns; the page scrolls vertically for taller six-row months. The same semantic Light/Dark resources color its surfaces, text, and focus indicators.

Month changes synchronously replace the date grid and cancel the preceding read. A generation guard discards any late result from the old month, including a backend that finishes after cancellation. The current grid stays visible during loading, on an empty result, or after a query error; Cancel and Refresh provide recovery. Reloading clears old monetary presentation until the new read succeeds. WPF makes no financial calculation and displays no combined-currency total. M13.5 adds Account and Currency controls while preserving this month-grid behavior.

## M13.3 — Daily and weekly presentation

The supplied screenshot guides the compact day number, prominent amount, Trade count, and Saturday weekly summary. `CalendarPnlSummary` retains the reader's original `ClosedTradeMetrics`; the ViewModel maps dates and weeks, not economics. Daily and weekly amounts and counts come directly from that data.

- Non-Saturday dates with closed Trades show an amount and count per historical currency, ordered by currency code. Empty dates show only their date. A genuine zero displays `0.00` with currency; unavailable economics display a dash with currency and retain the Trade count.
- Positive amounts use the shared success brush, negatives the danger brush, and zero/unavailable values neutral text. Active-month daily results have subtle matching backgrounds. Mixed-currency cells remain neutral overall, with independent currency bands. Saturday cells stay neutral around their separate weekly result.
- Adjacent-month dates are muted and their daily backgrounds are not tinted; their financial text remains readable. Today and keyboard focus retain accent borders in both themes.
- Each Saturday shows its date at the top, then only `Week 1`, `Week 2`, etc., the weekly P&L, and weekly Trade count, centered in the cell. The total includes all Monday–Sunday activity, including Saturday and Sunday, even across month boundaries. Selecting Saturday shows its own daily Trades in the details panel; no separate daily amount or count appears in its cell. Weekly currencies and their counts remain separate. An entirely empty loaded week shows a dash and `0 Trades`, not an invented currency or monetary zero.
- Concise tooltips and day-cell accessible descriptions provide exact unrounded amounts, estimated-Net provenance (commission/fees unknown), and incomplete coverage. No verbose explanation is placed inside the cells.
- The grid has a minimum width of 840 DIPs and horizontal scrolling at narrower widths. Wrapped text and variable row height accommodate separate currencies and weekly summaries without overlaying other cells.

Automated coverage checks signs, zero/empty/unavailable values, estimates, separate currencies, Saturday activity, Sunday contributions, and month-boundary totals. WPF layout/render tests cover Light and Dark at 1,100 DIPs/96 DPI and 640 DIPs/240 DPI. These are component renders, not interactive acceptance.

## M13.4 — Selected-day details and Trade navigation

Every date is selectable by click or Tab followed by Enter/Space, including adjacent-month dates and Saturdays. Selection has an accent outline and an accessible selected state. The month grid stays visible above a responsive details panel. Saturday selection queries Saturday's own closure date, never its Monday–Sunday summary. The panel shows the selected date, total closed-Trade count, and separate historical-currency daily metrics. Empty days show “No closed Trades” rather than a fabricated financial zero.

`TradingCalendarDayQuery(date, tradingAccountId?)` reuses `DashboardAnalyticsQuery` validation and New York midnight conversion. `ITradingCalendarDayReader.GetAsync` returns `TradingCalendarDayDetails`: ordered `TradeListItem` rows and `TradingCalendarDayCurrency` metrics. The fresh-context, no-tracking SQLite query filters `TradeBrowse` to fully closed Trades whose `ClosedAtUtc` lies in the half-open UTC interval for that local day. DST days therefore span 23 or 25 hours where applicable. It projects stored economics and historical pricing currency, with no paging or arbitrary result cap. A single batched execution-side/quantity query supplies peak Size through the same helper as the Trades list; it does not load screenshots or recalculate P&L from prices. Daily currency summaries reuse `DashboardMetricCalculator` over exactly those rows. Both reads forward cancellation and perform no database writes.

Rows are newest closure first, then Trade ID for ties. Each shows New York closing time (including UTC offset to distinguish DST overlap), Instrument, Account, direction, peak simultaneous Size, and Effective Net. Verified Net stays authoritative; unknown costs use the existing Gross-based estimate with a compact estimated label and accessible explanation. Unavailable amounts show a dash, and genuine zero stays numeric. View uses that row's Trade ID in the established Trades Details navigation, even if the Trade lies outside the current browse page. Stale row commands after a date/month change cannot open the previous selection.

Selecting another date cancels the previous read, clears old rows immediately, and discards late responses by generation and selected date. Changing month clears date selection; leaving Calendar cancels outstanding reads. Loading, Cancel, actionable error/Retry, and empty states retain a usable grid. Refresh reloads both the month and any selected day. The page scrolls vertically; the month grid and details table scroll horizontally at narrow widths without clipping View. Account and currency selection are added in M13.5 below.

Automated Application/SQLite tests cover DST bounds, ordering, estimates, unavailable/zero values, separate currencies, inactive accounts, Saturday versus Sunday weekly contributions, peak Size, cancellation, fresh reads, and 75 matching Trades without truncation. Desktop tests cover selection, adjacent dates, cancellation/retry, stale responses, exact View targets and navigation-away guards. Compiled WPF tests exercise mouse and Enter/Space selection and render both themes at 1,100 DIPs/96 DPI and 640 DIPs/240 DPI. These results do not constitute interactive desktop acceptance. Live mouse/keyboard selection, scrolling and View navigation still require an isolated Windows check if the desktop helper cannot expose the application window.

## M13.5 — Account and currency filters

The compact selectors above the grid default to **All accounts** and **All currencies**. Account options follow Dashboard conventions: readable names ordered by name/ID, inactive labels, and no inference of an Account from other features. Inactive Accounts with historical Trades remain included. Account is passed to both `TradingCalendarQuery` and `TradingCalendarDayQuery`, so filtering happens in their read paths before metrics or row presentation. Reference options are read afresh on month refresh; a selected Account missing from that read is retained as `(unavailable)` with an explicit instruction to choose another Account or All accounts. Its month/day data is cleared, never replaced by another Account's data.

Currency filtering selects the reader's existing historical-currency buckets rather than recalculating economics. The same captured selection filters daily and weekly metrics, selected-day currency summaries, and selected-day Trade rows/counts. All currencies keeps buckets separate without a cross-currency amount. A single-currency Saturday total includes only that currency's Monday–Sunday Trades; selecting Saturday still shows only its own date. Verified, estimated, unavailable, zero and empty semantics are unchanged.

Currency options list the current Account's visible-grid currencies and the retained currency, plus All currencies. A month with no matching Trades has an empty financial grid, but neither selector resets. Filters persist through Previous, Next, Today and date selection, and across leaving/re-entering Calendar. Changing either filter retains the selected date, clears old data immediately, cancels both in-flight loads, and refreshes month and day under the new selection. Generation guards reject late responses even from readers that ignore cancellation. No database, import, or stored-economics changes are involved.

Controls use the shared Light/Dark ComboBox style, named labels, keyboard-focusable selectors and wrapping at narrow widths. Automated tests cover defaults, inactive/unavailable Accounts, mixed currencies, filtered weekend counts, empty months, navigation persistence, day consistency and delayed filter races. Isolated migrated-SQLite coverage verifies Account-scoped month/day agreement and read-only behavior. Component layout/render checks cover both themes at 1,100 DIPs/96 DPI and 480 DIPs/240 DPI; these are not interactive acceptance.

M13.5 live acceptance was attempted with the Release executable and a synthetic migrated database. The desktop helper returned the application window, but screenshot capture timed out even after recovery, so live filtering remains unverified. Remaining Windows checks: choose an inactive Account and USD/EUR, select Saturday and compare its daily details with the Sunday-inclusive weekly summary, navigate to an empty month and back without filter reset, and check keyboard selection/wrapping in both themes at narrow/high-DPI sizes. Do not use the real journal for acceptance.
