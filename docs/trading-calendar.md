# Trading Calendar

## M13.1 — Read-only monthly data

`TradingCalendarQuery(year, month, tradingAccountId?)` selects a displayed New York calendar month. `null` Account means all accounts; an empty Account ID is invalid. `ITradingCalendarReader.GetAsync` returns `TradingCalendarMonth` with `MonthStart`, `GridStart`, `GridEnd`, the complete ordered `GridDates`, and one `TradingCalendarCurrency` per historical pricing currency with matching Trades. The grid begins on Monday on/before the first of the month and ends on Sunday on/after the last. It has four to six complete rows. For example, September 2026 includes August 31 through October 4. Adjacent-month days are marked by `IsInDisplayedMonth` but contribute normally to their visible week's totals. Even an empty month has a complete date grid and no fabricated currency bucket.

The reader delegates **one** inclusive grid-date query to `IDashboardAnalyticsReader`, which filters fully closed Trades by `ClosedAtUtc` using the existing DST-safe New York midnight boundaries. Open and partially exited Trades do not contribute. It does not use Trades-list paging, current Instrument pricing, source import TradeDay, or a retained snapshot. The underlying fresh-context query is no-tracking and projects authoritative persisted Gross, Net, and historical currency, not displayed prices or executions. No database writes are made; cancellation is forwarded to the SQL query and checked during shaping. An Account filter applies to every date and weekly summary. An unavailable Account ID yields an empty result, not another Account's Trades.

Each currency has ordered `TradingCalendarWeek` rows, each with seven `TradingCalendarDay` values and a Monday–Sunday `ClosedTradeMetrics` summary. Weekly totals and closed-Trade counts include **Sunday**, although the UI places the summary in that row's **Saturday cell**. Day and week metrics directly reuse M12's `DashboardMetricCalculator` output. There is no cross-currency monetary total. `Metrics == null` means **no closed Trades** for a day or week; `ClosedTradeCount == 0` is its convenience count, not a financial zero. A populated period with `EffectiveNet.Total == 0` is a genuine zero. `EffectiveNet.Total == null` for a populated period means complete numeric P&L is unavailable; `KnownSubtotal` and `Coverage` remain explicit partial evidence, not a total.

Effective Net uses authoritative Net when costs are known. For a closed Trade with unknown costs and known Gross, it uses Gross as an **estimate** without changing stored cost or Net nullability. `IsEstimated` and `EstimatedTradeCount` retain that provenance at both day and week scope. Unknown Gross cannot become a numeric estimate. Strict verified `Net` and its coverage remain available separately. All amounts retain full decimal precision; the Desktop formats amounts to two culture-aware decimal places without changing those values.

## M13.2 — Month grid and navigation

The Calendar destination now uses a retained `CalendarViewModel` and themed `CalendarView`. It initially selects the current **New York** month, shows a Monday–Sunday grid with four to six rows, and includes dimmed adjacent-month dates. Today has a small blue circular day-number marker. Previous, Next, and Today controls have accessible names; day cells are keyboard-focusable and announce their full date, adjacent-month/current-day state, and Saturday summary area. At narrow widths the grid scrolls horizontally instead of compressing seven columns; the page scrolls vertically for taller six-row months. The same semantic Light/Dark resources color its surfaces, text, and focus indicators.

Month changes synchronously replace the date grid and cancel the preceding read. A generation guard discards any late result from the old month, including a backend that finishes after cancellation. The current grid stays visible during loading, on an empty result, or after a query error; Cancel and Refresh provide recovery. Reloading clears old monetary presentation until the new read succeeds. WPF makes no financial calculation and displays no combined-currency total. M13.5 adds Account and Currency controls while preserving this month-grid behavior.

Stale row commands after a date/month change cannot open the previous selection.

Selecting another date cancels the previous read, clears old rows and chart series immediately, and discards late responses by generation and selected date. Changing month clears date selection; leaving Calendar cancels outstanding reads and closes its modal. Loading, Cancel, actionable error/Retry and empty states appear inside the dialog. Closing during loading cancels the presentation read without discarding the selected date. Refresh reloads both the month and any selected day, including an open dialog. ## M13.6 — Refresh, recovery and accessibility

The shared `MainWindowViewModel` Trade-commit handler notifies both Dashboard and Calendar on the owning WPF dispatcher. `CalendarViewModel.OnDataCommitted` refreshes only while active; re-entry always reads current data. Manual Trade creation, correction, closing/classification changes and deletion use the existing successful-write notification. Tradovate emits its existing Imported notification; TopstepX advances committed-data generations and emits its post-commit notification even when Import presentation was cleared during navigation. Each TopstepX generation is consumed once. NoChanges, Blocked, cancelled/rolled-back and failed imports emit no committed-Trade refresh. Persistence remains independent of UI callbacks; no schema or financial rules changed.

The named **Refresh Calendar month and selected day** action reloads both reads under independent generation guards, retaining the displayed month, selected date, Account and currency. A newer refresh cancels/invalidate older reads; even a reader that finishes after cancellation cannot publish an obsolete result. Cancel Calendar refresh stops both outstanding reads, while the details Cancel stops only the day read. Cancelled reads show retry guidance. Same-grid cells and their containers are retained so a refresh does not destroy keyboard focus; old financial values are cleared while their new read is pending, not presented as fresh data.

States remain distinct:

- Loading is announced without hiding the date grid or disabling navigation/filter recovery. Date cells expose their summary loading/loaded state, and the page exposes whether either read is busy.
- “No closed Trades in this month for the selected filters” describes the active month, not its adjacent-month cells. Adjacent activity remains visible but cannot suppress that message. A selected empty day separately shows “No closed Trades”.
- A day with closed Trades and a genuine zero stays populated with numeric zero and its count. A populated day with unavailable economics retains a dash and count. Estimated values keep their original reader provenance; no zero costs are invented.
- Month errors offer Refresh; day errors offer Retry. Neither is labelled an empty result. Dates and selectors remain usable. An unavailable Account remains selected with its explicit recovery message on repeated refresh, never silently falling back to All accounts.

Previous/Next/Today, Account/Currency, Refresh/Cancel, dates and row-specific View actions are keyboard reachable. Dates can be selected by Enter or Space or the UI Automation Invoke pattern; their accessible names identify the full date, adjacent/current month, today, selection and daily result or Saturday's Monday–Sunday summary. The Saturday cell remains weekly-only, while the separately named selected-date modal always describes that date's own daily Trades. Theme-aware keyboard-focus marker rings remain distinct from the accent selection ring around the day number. The existing scoped nested-scroll routing forwards an ordinary vertical gesture once to the movable page ancestor, leaves boundary input available to an outer viewer and preserves Shift+wheel and horizontal scrollbars; no global wheel handler was added.
